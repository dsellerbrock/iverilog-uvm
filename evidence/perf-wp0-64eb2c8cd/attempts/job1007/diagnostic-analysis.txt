Job 1007 reran the unchanged 64eb2c8cd benchmark snapshot with five interleaved pairs on DAN-DESKTOP, pinned to the same allowed CPU. All 18 workloads produced byte-identical reference/candidate outputs, and all samples stayed under the 10-second per-process cap. Hardware instructions remained unavailable, so task-clock was the primary metric.

The reference task-clock spread gate failed only for two short workloads: gate_strength (12.097%, 145.57–163.26 ms; median 146.23 ms) and multi_clock (45.041%, 30.93–45.22 ms; median 31.46 ms). In both cases, four of five task-clock samples clustered closely and one isolated sample was high. gate_strength run 2 also had a wall-time outlier (213.944 ms versus about 164 ms); multi_clock wall times remained within 1.62%. The other 16 reference workloads met the 5% task-clock spread gate.

This run does not establish stable measurements for the two short workloads. The next calibration revision increases their deterministic work while keeping both workloads below 10 seconds. No runtime or randomized-draw source changes are justified by these results.
